## A little about me

I’m a graduate of Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in Electrical & Electronic Engineering, where I was actively involved in student leadership and hands-on projects. I graduated as valedictorian with Highest Distinction.[^ntu-valedictorian]

Along the way, I’ve learned that I thrive in high-agency environments — places where ideas move fast, ownership matters, and systems can be continuously improved.

## Engineering & building

I joined Micron as a HVM Process Integration Engineer, using data analytics to identify problems and propose solutions.[^micron-problem-solving]

At NTU, I explored the design and optimisation of radio-frequency circuits[^project-design-and-optimization-of-radio-frequency-circuits-ntu] and helped build an education project about growing food locally for UKSAEI 2022.[^uksaei-2022-foreign-foragers-learn-grow-local]

Together with my friends, I started Learnr in March 2023 to explore better learning experiences through interactive content, discussion, and structured knowledge pathways.

With TINKRR, I’m designing a life OS that turns personal tracking into a coherent, gamified system: time, health, habits, and progress.

## The people along the way

Garage@EEE was a place to spark ideas with my friends and learn.[^garage-eee] I led the Enitio orientation programmes in 2021[^enitio2021] and 2023,[^enitio2023] bringing technology into the games and experiences we created for freshmen.

My university years also included leading the Machine Learning and Data Analytics Lab,[^mlda-eee] organising events with the NTU Buddhist Society,[^event-director-ntu-buddhist-society] and community work in Laos with NTU EEE Lead.[^ntu-eee-lead-laos]
